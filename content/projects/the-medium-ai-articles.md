These are four Medium posts that explain how current AI works in plain language. Together they give a top-level understanding of large language models (LLMs) to anyone getting into AI.

The series has paused. I stopped writing while preparing for my wedding in the second half of 2024, and since then I have spent my time on hands-on AI work rather than theory.

## The posts

1. [AI Simplified: Machine Learning Basics](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-simplified-machine-learning-basics-f579a90ddd0e). My favourite. It reduces neural networks to their most basic principles. It is probably also the best written, because I had years to understand and simplify neural networks, whereas I came to understand the transformer much more recently. Highly recommended for anyone getting into AI.
2. [AI Explained: How to Teach an Old Computer New Words](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-how-to-teach-an-old-computer-new-words-61d65a41e1b5). A simple, intuitive explanation of transformers, the T in ChatGPT. It takes examples from English and relates attention, the transformer's main idea, to simple grammar rules. In fairness, it is heavily inspired by [this 3Blue1Brown video](https://youtu.be/wjZofJX0v4M), but it covers more ground and is simpler.
3. [AI Explained: Objects as Vectors](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-objects-as-vectors-83c6c40c843f). The shortest and quickest to write; I thought of it as filler. Ironically, it is the most useful for anyone doing knowledge work, because it is a way of thinking that applies to any field, including life itself.
4. [The Endgame of LLMs: Virtual Employees](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/the-endgame-of-llms-virtual-employees-617071df4403). A plan for a true AI agent, written in August 2024, before agents were the hype. The plan and techniques are now completely obsolete; the field moved so fast they were out of date about 15 days after publishing. But the vision was big, and it still stands unconquered. I tried to build it and failed, and that is okay.

## Why I wrote them

I love teaching. Writing the posts forced me to understand the material deeply. And I wanted a body of work that showed I know AI.
