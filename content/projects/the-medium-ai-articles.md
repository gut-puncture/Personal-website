These are four Medium posts that explain how current AI works using plain language. Together, they give a top-level understanding of large language models to anyone getting into AI.

The series is paused. I stopped writing while preparing for my wedding in the second half of 2024. Since then, I have spent my time on hands-on AI work rather than theory.

## The posts

1. [AI Simplified: Machine Learning Basics](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-simplified-machine-learning-basics-f579a90ddd0e). My favourite post. It reduces neural networks to their most basic principles. It is probably my best written post because I had years to understand neural networks, whereas I learned about transformers more recently. I highly recommend it for anyone getting into AI.
2. [AI Explained: How to Teach an Old Computer New Words](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-how-to-teach-an-old-computer-new-words-61d65a41e1b5). A simple explanation of transformers, which is the T in ChatGPT. It uses examples from English and relates attention to simple grammar rules. It is heavily inspired by [this 3Blue1Brown video](https://youtu.be/wjZofJX0v4M), but it covers more ground and is simpler.
3. [AI Explained: Objects as Vectors](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-objects-as-vectors-83c6c40c843f). This was the shortest and quickest post to write, and I initially thought of it as filler. Ironically, it is the most useful post for anyone doing knowledge work. It teaches a way of thinking that applies to any field.
4. [The Endgame of LLMs: Virtual Employees](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/the-endgame-of-llms-virtual-employees-617071df4403). A plan for a true AI agent, written in August 2024 before agents became popular. The plan and techniques are now completely obsolete because the field moved so fast. They were out of date about 15 days after publishing. However, the vision was big, and it still stands unconquered. I tried to build it and failed, and that is okay.

## Why I wrote them

I love teaching. Writing these posts forced me to understand the material deeply. I also wanted a body of work that proved I understand AI.
