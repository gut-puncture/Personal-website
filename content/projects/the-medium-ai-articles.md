These are four posts on Medium that explain in plain language how current AI works. Together they give anyone who is getting into AI a top-level understanding of large language models, the kind of model behind ChatGPT.

The series is paused. I stopped writing while I was preparing for my wedding in the second half of 2024, and since then I have spent my time on hands-on AI work rather than on theory.

## The posts

1. [AI Simplified: Machine Learning Basics](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-simplified-machine-learning-basics-f579a90ddd0e). This is my favourite post, and it reduces neural networks to their most basic principles. It is probably also my best written post, because I had years to understand neural networks, whereas I learned about transformers much more recently. I highly recommend it to anyone who is getting into AI.
2. [AI Explained: How to Teach an Old Computer New Words](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-how-to-teach-an-old-computer-new-words-61d65a41e1b5). This post gives a simple explanation of transformers, which are the T in ChatGPT. It uses examples from English and relates attention, the main idea in a transformer, to simple rules of grammar. It is heavily inspired by [this 3Blue1Brown video](https://youtu.be/wjZofJX0v4M), but it covers more ground and is simpler.
3. [AI Explained: Objects as Vectors](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/ai-explained-objects-as-vectors-83c6c40c843f). This was the shortest and quickest post to write, and at first I thought of it as filler. Ironically, it is the most useful post for anyone doing knowledge work, because it teaches a way of thinking that applies to any field.
4. [The Endgame of LLMs: Virtual Employees](https://medium.com/@shaileshrana1995/the-endgame-of-llms-virtual-employees-617071df4403). This post sets out a plan for a true AI agent, and I wrote it in August 2024, before agents became popular. The plan and its techniques are now completely obsolete, because the field moved so fast that they were out of date about 15 days after I published them. The vision was big, however, and it still stands unconquered. I tried to build it and failed, and that is okay.

## Why I wrote them

I love teaching, and writing these posts forced me to understand the material deeply. I also wanted a body of work that proved I understand AI.
